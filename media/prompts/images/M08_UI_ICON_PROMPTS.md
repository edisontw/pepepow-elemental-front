# M08 Manual UI Icon Prompts

These prompts define optional final-art replacements for the current CSS/procedural HUD symbols. They are not gameplay dependencies. Keep the stable asset IDs unchanged when replacing placeholders.

## Shared style

Square 1:1 game UI icon, transparent background, bold readable silhouette at 32–64 px, stylized elemental RTS aesthetic, slightly beveled painted-metal / magical-material finish, strong value contrast, no text, no letters, no border frame, no watermark, centered object, consistent lighting from upper left.

## Resource icons

### `ui.icon.material`
Canonical filename: `ui-icon-material.webp`

A compact faceted amber-gold construction crystal combined with a small forged metal plate, conveying physical building material and industry, sturdy geometric silhouette, warm gold and muted steel, no coins, no text.

### `ui.icon.mana`
Canonical filename: `ui-icon-mana.webp`

A luminous violet-teal arcane droplet with a small inner star-like glow, magical energy resource, clean teardrop silhouette, saturated but not neon-clipped, no potion bottle, no text.

### `ui.icon.influence`
Canonical filename: `ui-icon-influence.webp`

A small strategic banner / command pennant with a radiant central sigil shape, conveying territorial authority and strategic influence, ivory-gold with restrained teal accent, no readable emblem or letters.

### `ui.icon.population`
Canonical filename: `ui-icon-population.webp`

Three simplified helmeted unit silhouettes grouped as a compact formation marker, readable as army capacity rather than a specific faction, pale steel with teal edge light, no faces, no text.

## Production notes

- Generate one icon per image, never as a contact sheet.
- Preserve transparent background where the generator supports it.
- Export final approved files as WebP with the canonical filenames above.
- Until manual approval, manifest status remains `NEEDS_MANUAL_GENERATION` and runtime keeps CSS/procedural placeholders.
